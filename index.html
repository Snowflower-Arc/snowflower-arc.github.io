Snowflower Arc

Snowflower Arc is a precise archive utility for macOS. It handles creation and extraction of compressed archives with unmatched speed, simplicity, and security. Designed for professionals who value efficiency without compromise.

Effortless Archiving

Create archives in 7z, Cpio, Tar, and Zip formats. Apply compression with Bz2, Gzip, LZ4, LZO, Snappy, XZ, or Zstandard. Fast, reliable, and tailored for macOS workflows.

How It Works

1. Drag and Drop

Select files or folders and drag them into Snowflower Arc. Choose your archive format and compression method.

2. Compress or Encrypt

Apply encryption if needed. Hit compress. Archives form in seconds, optimized for size and speed.

3. Extract Anywhere

Drag any supported archive to extract. Handles multi-format compression and disk images effortlessly.

4. Manage Seamlessly

Verify, preview, or re-archive on the fly. Native macOS integration keeps everything fluid.
